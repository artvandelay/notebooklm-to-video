# Title
India's DPDP Act: Privacy Law or Digital Control? | DPDPA 2023 & RTI

# Description
India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act promises greater privacy—but what kind of power structure does it create?

This four-part masterclass separates the legal framework from a provocative game-theory critique. It examines:

- The phased implementation of the DPDPA 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025
- Consent Managers and their relationship with Account Aggregators
- Breach reporting, compliance costs, and the ₹250 crore penalty framework
- State exemptions and institutional asymmetry
- Section 44(3), personal information, and the future of the Right to Information Act
- The constitutional challenge in Venkatesh Nayak v. Union of India

One host explains the law impartially. The other asks who gains leverage when consent, enforcement, and public information flow through this new architecture.

Primary references:
- Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023
- Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025
- Gazette notifications G.S.R. 843(E)–846(E)
- Justice K.S. Puttaswamy (Retd.) v. Union of India
- Right to Information Act, 2005
- Venkatesh Nayak v. Union of India, W.P.(C) 177/2026

This episode is educational commentary, not legal advice. Legal and institutional developments are presented as a snapshot from August 2026. Verify current legislation, notifications, and court records before relying on them.
